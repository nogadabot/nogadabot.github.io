# Choosing proxies

You do not have to buy proxies. Minting that goes straight to the contract does not need them. Consider them for site requests with many wallets, or for social tasks.

## What to look at when choosing

- Whether the IP is fixed. A rotating proxy that changes IP on every connection does not fit a site session
- Whether it is HTTP or HTTPS. The app does not accept SOCKS
- Whether it actually reaches the site you need. Enter that site in the **Test URL** field of the **Proxies** menu to try it
- Whether latency and failure rate are stable
- Region, number of simultaneous connections, and traffic limits

For social tasks, it is best to use one proxy per account. Proxies that share the same real IP count as one to the site.

Providers and prices change often. Test with a small amount first. To learn how to add them, see [Proxies](../setup/proxies.md).
