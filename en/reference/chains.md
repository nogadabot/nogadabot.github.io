## Public RPCs

Each chain has a built-in public RPC. The ones you put in the RPC menu are used first. Do not count on public RPCs as a fast path for a competitive mint.

## Disperse contracts

Most chains use the same default disperse contract. Bittensor has none.

## Showing and hiding

In the chain list under **Settings → Fire Engine**, you decide which chains appear in the pickers. Hiding a chain does not delete its saved RPCs. Some new chains and testnets are hidden at first. Turn them on if you need them.

For a chain that is not in the list, see [Add a custom chain](custom-chains.md).
